# K09: LocalDNS prerequisites and effects

K09 is traced from documentation and design, and nothing ran for it. The proposed verdict is `untestable`, for the inventory reason: LocalDNS changes the DNS path for every item. The [corrected write-up](https://github.com/collaborationwithothers/eventhubs-kafka-dotnet/blob/6942f20bd434804ddc2cbd7b7d276a3e9ab6bab3/docs/traces/K09.md) records the claim, its pinned sources and the trace across design, .NET code, Terraform, security, symptom, detection and runbook.

Proposed verdict: `untestable`.
