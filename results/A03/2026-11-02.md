# A03: producer connections were kept across a 420 s idle period with `metadata.max.age.ms=180000`, and not with 900000

In batch run 41534423570 on Event Hubs Premium, five fresh synthetic producers per configuration each produced one record, stayed idle for 420 s and produced again: C0 with `metadata.max.age.ms=900000`, the default in Microsoft's table, C1 with `180000`, Microsoft's recommendation, and C2 with `180000` and `topic.metadata.refresh.interval.ms=180000`; C0 and C1 left the refresh interval at the baseline 300000 ms. Counting only disconnects after a quiet period of at least 225000 ms, a harness threshold rather than a measurement, C0's connections were not kept in all five repeats (5 `closed_while_idle`) and C1's were kept in all five (5 `kept`), so the expected.yaml rules select `confirmed`, reason `microsoft_value_kept_connection`. The expected outcome, inferred from the librdkafka source, was that C1 would not be kept either; that difference is reported below as a finding.

A [correction note about the mutation publication gate](#correction-note-2026-11-03) was added on 2026-11-03.

Proposed verdict: `confirmed`.

## Repeat outcomes

The verifier judged every repeat under the [idle-observation judgement](../../docs/operations/verifier-contract.md#idle-observation-judgement). A disconnect is classed only by its quiet period: the smaller of `txidle` and `rxidle` in the statistics report before the disconnect. It counts as `idle_timed` when that period is at least 225000 ms. That threshold is a harness choice, not a measurement: Microsoft's stated 240000 ms idle limit minus one 15000 ms report interval. No report names who closed a connection. A repeat is `closed_while_idle` when an `idle_timed` disconnect falls in the idle window, `stale_on_resume` when one falls only in the resume window, and `kept` otherwise; the first two are `not_kept`.

## Finding: the result differs from the expected outcome

expected.yaml expects `not_kept` in C1 as in C0, an inference from `src/rdkafka.c` L2458-L2461 at librdkafka v2.16.0, where the periodic metadata refresh timer is started from `topic.metadata.refresh.interval.ms`. C0 was `not_kept`, as expected. C1 was `kept` in all five repeats. Under AGENTS.md "Verdict integrity" this difference is reported as a finding; the expected outcome is not changed.

The generator recomputes, from every statistics report in the candidate, the largest quiet period on any broker in each repeat, in whole milliseconds as the verifier computes it: 269728 ms to 269780 ms in C0, 179781 ms to 179901 ms in C1 and 165008 ms to 165010 ms in C2. Only C0's are above the 225000 ms threshold. In each C0 repeat the one disconnect followed a quiet period of 269728 ms to 269780 ms, with no request timeout, and no C1 or C2 repeat recorded a disconnect.
