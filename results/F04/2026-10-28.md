# F04: authorization errors and an assigned read with no outcome

The proposed F04 verdict is `undocumented`. Producing without a data-plane role returned broker error 29 in all five repeats, while consuming without a data-plane role yielded "no read and no error within the window", which is not an explicit denial. The synthetic workload covers 25 fresh-client repeats across five configurations, with successful C0 produce and C4 consume controls.

Proposed verdict: `undocumented`.

## Assessment against expected.yaml

**H0 and H4 hold.** C0 delivered the control marker in all five repeats. C4 read the exact control marker in all five repeats, with C0's successful candidate and receipt accepted as prerequisites.

**H1 is recorded only.** C1's five produce attempts without a data-plane role on `document-state` ended with broker error 29, nonfatal. This is the measured producer rejection under the tested identity, target and client version.

**H2 is recorded only.** C2's five assigned reads are published as exactly "no read and no error within the window". This class establishes neither an explicit authorization rejection nor an authorization success. The accepted seed prerequisite prevents an absent setup record from being treated as this finding.

**H3 is recorded only.** C3's five produce actions with a storage-resource token ended with local error -169, nonfatal. Its zero broker counts and authentication error class belong to the wrong-resource-token configuration; they do not replace the C1 or C2 observations.

**Verdict.** The approved `undocumented_when` rule requires successful C0 and C4 controls and decided C1, C2 and C3 classes, with the fixed C2 wording above. Those conditions hold, and no C1-C3 success triggered the security-finding stop rule. The proposed verdict is therefore `undocumented`, matching the verifier's item assessment in C4. The earlier configuration candidates have null item assessments because the final configuration combines the accepted candidates and matching judge receipts. This is the approved verdict classification for the measured classes, not a claim that no documentation discusses authorization.
