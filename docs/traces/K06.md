# K06: spot eviction notice against the termination grace period

Two Microsoft pages describe the warning a spot VM gets before eviction, and they disagree on its bound. This item is traced from documentation and design, not tested, because the harness runs on normal node pools and no spot eviction happens. The main finding is the conflict itself: one page says "up to" 30 seconds and the other says "at a minimum of" 30 seconds, and these cannot both describe the same bound.

Sources were read on 2026-10-27. Line numbers refer to the linked commit or tag. "Inference" marks a statement that no cited source makes.

## Claim

The [Azure Spot Virtual Machines overview](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/azure-compute-docs/blob/509cdb5e28e4759acd0c2ef5185e0e71a8c49362/articles/virtual-machines/spot-vms.md) (ms.date 02/06/2026) says at line 33 that in-VM notifications through Azure Scheduled Events "are delivered on a best effort basis up to 30 seconds prior to the eviction".

The [spot eviction guide](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/architecture-center/blob/888968f7336824affc11378f181f727a84045e13/docs/guide/spot/spot-eviction-content.md) (ms.date 01/28/2025, set in spot-eviction.yml) says at line 82: "Azure sends out the `Preempt` signal to all VMs at a minimum of 30 seconds before they're evicted." Both pages describe the Scheduled Events notice before an eviction.

"Up to 30 seconds" is a ceiling. "At a minimum of 30 seconds" is a floor. They cannot both be the bound on one notice. The conflict is the finding. No cited source settles which page is right.

## Why it is traced, not tested

The inventory reason in [docs/blueprint.md](../blueprint.md) section 12 is "harness uses normal pools (ADR-008)". ADR-008 lists the consequence "Spot evictions cannot masquerade as client failures", so the build has no spot pool to evict.

A test would need a real spot eviction, which Azure decides and a scenario cannot trigger. Inference: even on a spot pool, the notice time could not be measured without an eviction, so the two pages could not be compared against a run.

## Trace

- Design: ADR-008 uses normal pools so that spot evictions cannot masquerade as client failures. The blueprint section 3 "AKS cluster" repeats it: normal, not spot, capacity.
- .NET code: none here. Worker shutdown (flush, close, commit on revoke) is the subject of inventory item C18, not this one.
- Terraform: every pool sets `scale_set_priority = "Regular"` ([aks.tf](https://github.com/collaborationwithothers/eventhubs-kafka-dotnet/blob/39e2cbba73f28855d3d0bbc3511d977294b8ba09/infra/cluster/aks.tf) lines 37, 46 and 53).
- Kubernetes: the job template sets `terminationGracePeriodSeconds` from the execution bounds ([jobs.yaml](https://github.com/collaborationwithothers/eventhubs-kafka-dotnet/blob/39e2cbba73f28855d3d0bbc3511d977294b8ba09/charts/parchwell/templates/jobs.yaml) line 47). The value is 30 seconds ([values.yaml](https://github.com/collaborationwithothers/eventhubs-kafka-dotnet/blob/39e2cbba73f28855d3d0bbc3511d977294b8ba09/charts/parchwell/values.yaml) line 83), and the chart test asserts 30 ([test_chart.py](https://github.com/collaborationwithothers/eventhubs-kafka-dotnet/blob/39e2cbba73f28855d3d0bbc3511d977294b8ba09/charts/parchwell/tests/test_chart.py) line 119).
- Security: none. The item changes no identity, network or credential setting.
- Symptom: Inference: if "up to 30 seconds" is right, the notice can be shorter than a 30-second grace period, so a pod that uses its whole grace period can be cut short by the eviction. If "at a minimum of 30 seconds" is right, the grace period fits.
- Detection: the AKS [node auto-drain page](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/azure-aks-docs/blob/1b2c1f91df475f8cb46f72cbf3ee125241523be8/articles/aks/node-auto-drain.md) (ms.date 03/30/2026) lists the `Preempt` event at line 29 with the description "The spot VM is being deleted." and the action "Cordon and drain." Inference: AKS starts the drain from that event, and the page does not say whether the drain gives a pod its full grace period.
- Runbook: the spot eviction guide says at line 110: "The VM shutdown process should be less than 30 seconds". Inference: on any spot pool, finish flush, close and commit well inside 30 seconds. Not tested.
